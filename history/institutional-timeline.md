# Timeline and institutional linkages

- 2018–2019: WEF Known Traveller Digital Identity announced; later reported never to have launched. Planned != operational.
- January 2020: WEF announces Digital Currency Governance Consortium.
- June 2020: Great Reset publicly launched.
- September 2020: WEF corporate stakeholder-capitalism metrics introduced with major firms.
- 2021: WEF DCGC white-paper series.
- 2023: WEF central bank digital currency interoperability guidance.
- 2024: EU digital identity regulation and platform-work directive adopted.
- July 2025: NIST SP 800-63 revision 4 published.
- 2025–2026: BIS wholesale tokenization experiments, distinct from retail labor credits.
- October 2026: PHP2Shell disclosure, patching, precautionary outages and emergency-alert impact.

The timeline is a **chronology**, not an asserted causal chain. Trace authorship, citations, procurement and implementation decisions to establish influence.
