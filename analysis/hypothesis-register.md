# Hypotheses / alternatives

**H1 — Integrated economic access gates.** Digital identity, payments and work management could be joined into more consequential eligibility systems. Individual components operational; full unified deployment not demonstrated.

**H2 — WEF influence.** WEF plans and technical groups may have influenced specific later standards and implementations. Public proposals documented; direct causal path requires citation of later adopting decisions.

**H3 — AI tokens as economic prototype.** Metered AI billing and results-based work share account, quota and performance concepts. Deliberate universal labor experiment is not established.

**H4 — Security consolidation after PHP2Shell.** Major vulnerabilities may drive increased central security-provider dependence. This remains prospective without post-incident policy, procurement or contract evidence.

**Competing explanations:** security engineering, fraud controls, ordinary business accounting, public-service efficiency, decentralized adoption, non-discrimination laws, abandoned pilots.

**Rule:** institutional overlap, a common date, or similar architecture is insufficient evidence of coordinated intent.
